      How to play

      Find the mystery country. Every guess lights up on the globe, coloured by how close it is: the hotter the colour, the closer you are. Guesses are unlimited.

      Far awayVery closeFound
      • Daily. One mystery country a day, the same for the whole family. It counts toward your streak and the leaderboard, and a new one arrives at midnight.
      • Unlimited. A new random country whenever you want, optionally inside one region. It has its own leaderboard and never touches your daily streak.
      • Capitals. The mystery is a capital city. Guess capitals (“Paris — France”); the distance is measured city to city and each guess is a dot on the globe.
      • Blind. Hides the map: only the sea and the places you have guessed are drawn. Switch it on or off with the eye button at any time.

      Distances

      For countries, distance is the shortest gap between the two borders, so a neighbour is 0 km away and painted deep red without being the answer. Tiny countries get a dot so you can find them.

      Territories such as Greenland or Puerto Rico can be guessed for their distance, but are never the answer. The list marks them.

      Sharing

      Your result shares as squares, never names: 🟩 found · 🟥 under 1,500 km · 🟧 under 3,750 km · 🟨 under 7,500 km · ⬜ farther.

      The globe

      Drag to spin, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap a guessed country to see its name. With the globe focused, the arrow keys turn it and +/− zoom. Press / to jump to the guess box.
